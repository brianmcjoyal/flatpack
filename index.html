Things people are building today

Nursery and baby furniture

These guides work differently, on purpose. We went through the CPSC recall database and read the manufacturers' own manuals for this category. Two things stood out. The most repeated structural failure in cribs is the mattress support — the four bolts you can never see once the mattress is in. And every nursery manual says to inspect the product "periodically" without once saying how often or what to look at.

So every nursery guide here carries the safety standard that applies, a prompt to check your model against the recall list, and a re-check schedule — the thing the manuals leave out.

How this works

Hundreds of thousands of different pieces of furniture are sold online, and it feels like each one needs its own manual. It doesn't. A Zinus bed, an Amazon Basics bed and a Novogratz bed are the same six moves. Every cam-lock dresser on earth — IKEA, Sauder, Ameriwood, South Shore — is the same twelve steps.

So instead of chasing a manual per product, Flatpack works out which of – assembly patterns your item uses, adds anything specific to – brands' hardware, and walks you through it. Type in a product we've never heard of and it still works, because the hardware underneath is the same.

Every guide gives you: a three-minute animated walkthrough you can pause and step through, the full written version in plain English, the tools you actually need and why, what to do when a part doesn't fit, and the safety step that matters — which for most furniture is anchoring it to the wall.

What we don't cover

Being straight with you about the edges. Cribs and nursery furniture used to be on this list and are now covered in depth — these are what is genuinely still out of scope:

    Flatpack is a reference guide, not the manufacturer. Where your manual and this guide disagree, your manual wins — it knows your exact model. For cribs, bunk beds and anything a child sleeps in or climbs on, the manufacturer's instructions and their current safety notices are the authority.

    Missing a part? Nearly every manufacturer ships replacement hardware free. Search their site for “replacement parts” with your model number to hand.
